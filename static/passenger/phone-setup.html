Checking the local setup…

  1. 01

    Get the demo certificate

    This public certificate lets your phone recognise the bus computer. Download it only for your own demo device.

    Download public certificate ↓
    SHA-256 · compare with this page on the host computer

    The API key stays on the computer. You do not enter it on your phone.

  2. 02

    Choose to trust it on your phone

    Check the fingerprint with the host computer first. Installation and trust are choices you make in your phone’s settings.

    iPhone or iPad +
    1. Open the downloaded certificate and install its profile in Settings. Look for Profile Downloaded, or General → VPN & Device Management.
    2. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
    3. Enable full trust for BusGuard temporary demo CA.
    Apple’s certificate trust instructions ↗
    Android +
    1. In Settings, search for Install a certificate or Encryption & credentials. Menu names vary by phone.
    2. Choose the CA certificate option and select the downloaded BusGuard-demo-root.cer.
    3. Confirm installation only after matching the fingerprint. Your screen lock may be required; managed phones can restrict this.
    Google’s certificate settings reference ↗

    Remove BusGuard temporary demo CA from your phone after the demonstration.

  3. 03

    Open voice and say hello

    Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. Open the secure passenger app, tap Start listening, and allow microphone access. Then say “Hey BusGuard”.

    Open secure passenger app ↗

    The page should open without a certificate warning. If it shows one, return to the setup steps or ask the operator to check the address and certificate.

Keep the app open while you travel.

Voice and arrival alerts work while the page is active. While listening is enabled, microphone audio goes to OpenAI, including while waiting for “Hey BusGuard”. Text controls remain available.

You opened this page using localhost. On your phone, use the bus computer’s Wi-Fi address instead.
